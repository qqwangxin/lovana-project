Our beginnings

It started with showing up for each other.

Between 2022 and 2025, we kept finding one another at Burning Man—building together, making art, and looking out for each other through rain, mud, and dust. Trust grew through those shared experiences.

We wanted to make a small community where people could feel seen and cared for, and help create that feeling for someone else. That became the spirit of Lovana & HealAF.

Everyone had a hand in making it happen: building, cooking, offering something to visitors, and packing it all away. The work was part of how we became a community.

A little warmth on the playa

A bowl of tofu.
A reason to stay.

We wanted to bring the warmth of Chinese street life to the desert: food still steaming, a kind word, and a place where someone passing by could stop and feel welcome.

At HealAF, that took shape through freshly made savory tofu, air cupping sessions, and time spent together. Food and cultural practices became ways to meet, share stories, and care for one another.

That spirit of hospitality is part of what we hope to carry into every Lovana gathering.

Beyond the desert

Carrying that feeling forward.

Lovana Project is a nonprofit based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Rooted in Chinese hospitality and Asian cultural traditions, we create opportunities for people of every background to connect through public art, cultural exchange, music, and well-being.

Our founding community’s past work includes The Transcendental Water, a participatory art experience at Burning Man in 2025. Our host community has brought people together through yoga and cacao gatherings. We’re now developing more public yoga sessions, snowboarding and tea ceremony teaching for children and families with low incomes, and Blossom, our proposed 2027 art project.

We’re still at the beginning. The next chapter will be shaped by the people who join in.
