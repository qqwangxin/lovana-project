Privacy

This website introduces Lovana Project and our community programs. It does not currently include visitor accounts, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or forms collecting personal information.

Website hosting

This site is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the website. GitHub’s privacy statement applies to that processing.

Donations and external links

When a secure donation link becomes available, payments and donor information will be handled by the donation provider. Its privacy policy and terms apply to its checkout. We may receive the information donors share with our organization to acknowledge gifts and maintain donation records.
